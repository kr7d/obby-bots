# MARS INVASION MODE

Completing M4 will unlock the MARS INVASION and a gun. MARS INVASION replaces 6 *regular stages* with special stages that randomly appear and rotate. Only completed stages will be able to be played in the MARS INVASION MODE. M8 is a MARS INVASION EXCLUSIVE STAGE and is only be available after completing all other MARS INVASION stages, staying until it's completed for the first time. MARS INVASION stages will appear every 30 minutes, randomly replacing an already completed stage. Incomplete MARS INVASION stages will have slightly increased odds of appearing. The replaced stage will not be able to have bots run on until the MARS INVASION is cleared or rotates and other players bots in the stage will be hidden. Playing the stage prevents the stage from rotating but does not stop the timer. 

These stages have MARS PEOPLE that chase the player once the player is in their proximity, coming into contact with a MARS PEOPLE will result instant death. Players can use the gun to destroy MARS PEOPLE by clicking on MARS PEOPLE while holding the gun (only in MARS INVASION stages). It will automatically aim, then fire after a short delay. The player will be slowed down during the sequence. The gun has a cooldown of 2 seconds and takes 1 second to fire. Bullets travel instantaneously, requires line of sight, and kill in one shot. 

## STAGE COMPLETION

Completing a MARS INVASION stage will reward the player with various items. The first 3 completions grant higher quality loot, excluding Stage 8.

Stage 1: 2 TIER 2 MARS INVASION CHESTS on first completions, then 1 TIER 1 MARS INVASION CHESTS on subsequent completions.

Stage 4: 4 TIER 2 MARS INVASION CHESTS on first completions, then 2 TIER 2 MARS INVASION CHEST on subsequent completions.

Stage 5: 1 TIER 3 MARS INVASION BUNDLE on first completions, then 3 TIER 2 MARS INVASION CHESTS on subsequent completions.

Stage 6: 1 TIER 4 MARS INVASION BUNDLE on first completions, then 2 TIER 3 MARS INVASION BUNDLES on subsequent completions.

Stage 7: 2 TIER 4 MARS INVASION BUNDLES on first completions, then 1 TIER 4 MARS INVASION BUNDLES on subsequent completions. 

Stage 8: 3 TIER 5 MARS INVASION BUNDLES and Advanced Raygun on first completion, then 1 TIER 5 MARS INVASION BUNDLES on subsequent completions. 

## STAGE 8

GREEN VULITHOR boss fight. M8 only appears after the first 5 MARS INVASION stages are complete. It is a multi stage fight where the player has to shoot VULITHOR 3 times.

## STAGE 8 COMPLETION

Completing Stage 8 guarantees GREEN VULITHOR. The player also gets an Advanced Raygun (can swap guns when not in a stage) that fires a very fast projectile that pierces MARS PEOPLE and bounces off objects (20 bounces). It fires instantly and has a cooldown of 5 seconds. Aiming is manually controlled by the cursor and has a laser. Stages are designed to incorporate the bouncing mechanic to allow for multiple MARS PEOPLE to be killed very quickly in a single shot in order to be able to complete the stage faster. 

Stage 8 completion also decreases the time it takes for MARS INVASION stages to rotate (30 min > 10 min) and increases the number of concurrent MARS INVASION stages (1 > 2). Both stages will rotate at the same time.
